Real-Time Video, Screen Share & Shared Canvas

Clear meetings built for live engineering and team collaboration.

AVORA brings peer-to-peer WebRTC video and audio, instant screen sharing, protected meeting file exchange, and a synchronized HTML5 whiteboard into a single browser workspace.

Everything required to run a working session

Every tool in AVORA is wired to persistent server state and real-time room sockets—no external plugins or desktop downloads required.

01. Multi-Participant WebRTC Video & Audio

Direct encrypted video calls with instant track switching

Negotiate offers, answers, and ICE candidates over authenticated Socket.IO rooms. Toggle microphones and cameras cleanly, monitor active participants, and switch into browser screen sharing without dropping the call.

02. Collaborative Whiteboard

Live HTML5 Canvas synchronized across the room

Draw freehand diagrams, add text annotations, switch ink colors and stroke widths, erase cleanly, and recover the exact board state when participants join late.

03. Screen Sharing

Full-screen, window, or tab capture

Present design files or live code using the browser Screen Capture API and automatically restore your camera feed when sharing ends.

04. Protected File Exchange

Room-scoped uploads with MIME validation

Upload documents, archives, or media inside a meeting with live progress tracking. Downloads are verified server-side for authorized participants only.

05. Governance & Administration

JWT access control and audit visibility

Host controls to end sessions, user profile management, meeting history records, and an admin console with account activation controls.

How a session flows on AVORA

From account creation to post-meeting records, every step operates over authenticated REST APIs and room-scoped WebSockets.

01

Create your account

Register with validated credentials backed by Django password hashing and JWT access/refresh tokens.

02

03

Collaborate live

Stream camera and microphone audio, share your screen, exchange files, and sketch together on the whiteboard.

04

Review history

Access past meeting records, uploaded files, and participant logs anytime from your personalized dashboard.
